## The elephant in the classroom

It is not a future threat: it is the present. The same free tools that draft emails draft
history essays, solve math worksheets and translate English homework. Students have known
this longer than many adults. The question is no longer whether they will use it, but what
we do about it.

## Why prohibition fails

Three reasons. First: **there is no reliable detector**. Programs that promise to detect
AI text fail in both directions — they accuse the innocent (especially those not writing
in their first language) and miss anyone who edits a little. Second: **it punishes
honesty** — the student who asks "may I use it for this?" ends up worse off than the one
who uses it in secret. Third: **it wastes the classroom**. School is exactly the place to
learn to use a powerful tool with judgment, instead of learning it alone on the internet.

## What is working

Teachers across the region are trying approaches that share one idea: change what gets
assessed. Some concrete examples:

- **Grade the process**: intermediate drafts, logs of how the work was done, oral defense
  of what was written. AI can write the essay; it cannot defend it for you.
- **Ask for what AI does poorly**: personal experience, interviews with family, observation
  of the local environment, work in the community's language. A model trained on the
  English-language internet doesn't know what happened in your town last week.
- **Use it in plain sight**: ask it for a text in class and correct it together. Finding
  the machine's mistake teaches more than memorizing the lesson — it teaches doubt.
- **Agree on clear rules per assignment**: "here yes, citing it; here no, and here is
  why." Clarity turns the topic from cheating into method.

## The heart of the matter

AI literacy is now simply literacy: understanding what a model does, when it lies, whose
data it holds. Students who learn to use it with judgment today will be the adults it
cannot fool tomorrow. That is the classroom's job — and no detector does it for us.
